It doesn't seem that complicated. If one tries to be as good as possible and try to avoid bad as possible, it leads to the best life someone can live.  
People like to live successfully, rich, famous, hero, champion, family man, actor, doctor, scientist, leader, engineer, entrepreneur, billionaire, president, king, saint, prophet. But who wants to live god?  
God is better than any other. God is the best. Why does one not want to live god? not the omnipotent god, not the uncreated and everlasting god, not the creator, preserver, destroyer god, not a tyrant, oppressor, slave driver god.  
not even a righteous and just god. righteousness and justice is good, not the best. God is more! God is kind, wise, generous, forgiver and merciful. God is the collection of all good and void from all bad. God sits and watches and doesn't interfere! God loves all, even the bad ones, either if punish them or forgives them. How is it possible God created something bad, something that doesn't like it? someone must aim high, highest, to reach its highest and what is higher than god?  
The world is full of creatures. Where is Creator? Where is God? Be God!
